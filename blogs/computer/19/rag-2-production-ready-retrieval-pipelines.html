RAG 2.0 (Next-Generation RAG)

From demos to reliable retrieval systems

RAG 2.0 is less about adding a vector DB and more about building a measurable retrieval pipeline with quality controls at every stage.

1) Why first-generation RAG failed

Many systems shipped without source trust scoring, freshness guarantees, or evaluation harnesses, producing plausible but unverifiable outputs.

2) Better retrieval architecture

  • Hybrid search (BM25 + dense vectors)
  • Domain-specific chunking with semantic boundaries
  • Cross-encoder reranking before generation

3) Grounding and citation quality

Require source references, reject low-confidence context, and expose evidence snippets to users for transparency.

4) Evaluation in CI

  • Golden question sets by domain
  • Faithfulness and citation-coverage metrics
  • Regression gates for retrieval precision@k

5) Operations and governance

Use data lineage, access controls, and retention policies. Treat embeddings and prompts as versioned artifacts, not ad hoc configuration.
