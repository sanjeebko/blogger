Platform Engineering with Internal Developer Portals

Turning platform complexity into self-service workflows

Internal developer portals centralize service catalogs, golden paths, and operational ownership so teams can ship faster with fewer tickets.

1) Why portals matter

Without a portal, platform knowledge lives in tribal docs and Slack threads. Portals make standards discoverable and repeatable.

2) Core capabilities

  • Service catalog with ownership and dependencies
  • Scaffolding templates for new services
  • Scorecards for reliability, security, and docs health

3) Golden paths

Provide opinionated defaults (CI, logging, alerts, IaC modules) while allowing escape hatches for advanced teams.

4) Measuring impact

Track lead time to first deploy, onboarding time, and ratio of self-service actions vs manual platform tickets.

5) Adoption strategy

Start with one high-traffic workflow (new API service), prove value, then expand templates and governance gradually.
