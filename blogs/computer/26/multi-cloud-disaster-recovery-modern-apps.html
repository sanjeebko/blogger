Multi-Cloud Disaster Recovery for Modern Apps

Designing resilience for regional and provider outages

Disaster recovery succeeds when architecture, data replication, and operational drills are designed together.

1) Define business targets first

Set service-specific RTO and RPO values. Not all systems need active-active recovery; classify by business criticality.

2) Choose recovery topology

  • Active-passive for cost-efficient resilience
  • Active-active for near-zero downtime workloads
  • Pilot-light for controlled warm starts

3) Data strategy

Use immutable backups, cross-cloud replication, and periodic restore verification to avoid false confidence.

4) Traffic and identity failover

Plan DNS/traffic-manager cutover, cert/key distribution, and identity federation availability in both clouds.

5) Run chaos-style recovery drills

Practice failovers quarterly and capture gaps in runbooks, access, dependencies, and communication flow.
