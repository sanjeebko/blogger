Green Software Engineering for High-Scale Systems

Performance, cost, and carbon optimization together

Sustainable systems are efficient systems. Lower compute waste usually reduces both cloud bill and carbon intensity.

1) Carbon-aware architecture basics

Measure workload intensity by region and time, then place flexible jobs where and when cleaner energy is available.

2) Efficient software patterns

  • Right-size compute and storage tiers
  • Reduce redundant data movement
  • Prefer batching for non-latency-critical tasks

3) Green SRE metrics

Track energy per request, CPU idle waste, and carbon per transaction alongside latency and error rates.

4) Product-level tradeoffs

Use quality-of-service tiers to balance user experience and resource intensity during peak load.

5) Governance and reporting

Publish sustainability scorecards by service to create ownership and continuous improvement loops.
