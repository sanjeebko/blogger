## Style Guidelines for All Images - Heart Health:
- **Overall Style**: Professional, clinical yet reassuring medical illustration
- **Color Palette**: Medical reds (#D32F2F) for cardiovascular structures, professional blues (#1565C0), healing greens (#388E3C), white, and light backgrounds
- **Mood**: Health-focused, evidence-based, reassuring without causing alarm
- **Quality**: High-resolution, anatomically accurate, suitable for healthcare and medical education
- **Elements**: Accurate cardiac anatomy where appropriate, realistic medical equipment, clear data visualizations, heart rate monitoring elements
- **Composition**: Clear visual hierarchy, educational infographics, before/after comparisons, risk/protective factor balancing
- **Audience**: General public concerned about heart health, cardiac patients, healthcare professionals
